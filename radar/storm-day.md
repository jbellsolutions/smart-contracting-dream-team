# Storm-day playbook

When MetService has a heavy-rain watch or warning for Christchurch or Canterbury, more drains block and more sections flood within a day or two. That's the best time to be helpful and visible. Claude fills this in and Jesse picks what to do.

```
Storm watch · <warning name> · <area> · <from> to <until>

Before it hits (pick any):
1. Social tip post (business page or personal): "Heavy rain's on its way to Christchurch.
   Three quick checks before it hits: clear leaves off your gully trap grate, check your
   downpipes aren't blocked, and if your soak pit flooded last time, get it looked at before
   the next one." (Draft is in your words. No sales pitch, no link.)
2. Partner heads-up: a short note to each active property-manager partner offering a quick
   CCTV check for any rental that flooded or backed up last time. (Drafted for you to send
   from your own email, or ask Revenue Partner to send through its yes-gate.)
3. Radar: run the radar the day after the rain. The asks come in once people see the water.

After it passes:
4. Run the radar (lane 1) and check partner referrals.
```

Rules: no fear-selling, no "act now" and no made-up numbers. Only send a partner note to partners who signed. Claude here doesn't keep the partner list: ask the Revenue Partner in Slack "which partners are signed or active?" Never send to prospects; prospects are the Revenue Partner's job. No fee or GST talk in a storm note.
