## Group setup (Claude runs this with Jesse once)

1. Ask: "Which local Facebook groups are you already in, such as suburb community pages, Christchurch buy/sell/recommend groups, landlord or property groups, or renovation groups?"
2. If Jesse isn't sure, open `https://www.facebook.com/groups/joins/` in his browser (read only) and list the groups he's in that look local to Canterbury. Show him the list and let **him** pick up to 8.
3. Suggest the kinds of groups worth joining if the list is thin: big Christchurch community noticeboards, his own suburb and the neighbouring ones, "recommend a tradie" groups, Christchurch landlord and property investor groups, and renovation groups. **Don't join any of them.** Give Jesse the names and he joins if he wants. Groups that ban business posts are still fine to read, and a helpful reply from a personal profile is usually welcome. Follow each group's rules.
4. Write the chosen groups into the table above (name, link, why, today's date).
