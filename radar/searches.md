# Radar searches and tiers

These come from the three Christchurch drainage searches in Justin's hosted Lead Radar (pipes, stormwater, earthquake), rewritten for searching inside Facebook groups. Rotate through them. Each run uses 3 or 4 terms per group, so every term gets covered over a few runs.

## Search terms

**Pipes and blockages**
- blocked drain
- drainlayer (also try "drain layer")
- gurgling
- sewer smell (also try "smells like sewage")
- tree roots drain
- earthenware (also try "clay pipes")
- slow draining
- drain camera (also try "CCTV drain")

**Stormwater and sections**
- soak pit (also try "soak hole")
- flooding backyard (also try "pooling water")
- boggy section (also try "soggy lawn")
- downpipe
- gully trap

**Earthquake, insurance and buying**
- EQC drain (also try "NHC drain")
- earthquake drain
- pre purchase drain (also try "drain inspection")
- LIM drainage

**Recommendation asks (for these, check the post is about drains)**
- recommend plumber
- recommend drainage

## Tiers

| Tier | What it looks like | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | Asking for someone to come and fix or check drains now, with Canterbury evidence (group, suburb or place name) | "Can anyone recommend a drainlayer in Hornby? Tree roots have cracked our pipe" |
| **Medium** | Asking for recommendations or advice, with clear drain symptoms | "Gurgling drain and a smell in the laundry, who do people use?" |
| **Low** | Thinking about it, venting or DIY with no ask | "Ugh, soak pit flooded again". Log it, but give no draft |

**Jesse's priority** (Oct 7 call): larger residential repair work, like relays, root damage, broken earthenware, earthquake damage and stormwater problems. Inspection-only, new-build and civil jobs rank lower. If a post sounds like a big repair, say so in "Why it fits".

## Leave out (don't log)
- Other drainlayers, plumbers or tradies advertising ("we offer", "DM me for rates", "book now")
- Job ads and people looking for work
- Plumbing only, like toilet repairs, hot water cylinders and tapware, unless drains are mentioned too
- Anywhere outside Canterbury (watch out for Christchurch in Dorset, UK)
- Council rates, council tax and anything already answered by Smart Contracting
- Anything already in `data/radar.csv`
