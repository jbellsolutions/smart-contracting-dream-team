# Jesse's guide

Everything here works by talking. No commands to learn.

## Two places, two jobs

| | The Revenue Partner (in Slack) | The Workshop (Claude Code on your PC) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Your AI agent on the Orgo computer | Claude, opened on this folder in the Claude app (Code tab) |
| What it does | Cold emails for your tick (as now), plus: call cards when someone replies warmly, signing partners, their campaigns, referrals, the 5% fee, payouts, Friday scoreboard | Finds homeowners asking for drain help, makes partner packs, preps your calls, upgrades and fixes the Revenue Partner |
| When it works | On its own schedule, and when you message it | Only when you open it and ask |
| Partner records | **Keeps all of them** | Never touches them |

## Everyday things to say

**To the Revenue Partner, in Slack**
- "any replies?" → call cards for anyone keen
- "called Kate at Westside, keen, first campaign in November" → it sets them up as a partner and drafts the welcome
- "<customer> rang, came from <partner>" → logs the referral
- "<customer>'s invoice is paid, 12,000 before GST" → works out the partner's fee
- "what do we owe partners?" → this month's payout list (you pay; it never does)
- "how are we going?" → the scoreboard
- "slow down" / "go again" → pauses or restarts new partner campaigns when you're flat out
- "turn on call cards" → switches on a routine (everything starts off)

**To the Workshop, in Claude Code**
- "run the radar" · "partner pack for a building inspector" · "prep a call with Kate" · "upgrade the Revenue Partner" · "it's gone quiet"

## Opening the Workshop
1. Open the Claude app and click **Code**.
2. Choose this folder (`smart-contracting-dream-team`). The first time, it asks whether to trust the folder: say yes. That lets it use the settings in this folder, which stop it from deleting files or pushing anything online.
3. Say "set me up" the first time. After that, just say what you want.

## Turning on the browser
The radar reads Facebook groups you're already in, through your own browser. Two ways, either is fine:
- **The Claude app's own browser:** in Claude Code, when it asks to open a browser, allow it. It's the one that worked for Orgo on Oct 13.
- **Claude in Edge:** install the Claude extension in Edge and sign in with the **same** Claude account you use for work. If it's signed in with a different account, it won't connect.
Log into Facebook yourself in whichever one it uses. Claude never types your password.

## The approval questions
Every question you get asks plainly what will happen and how to answer, for example:
> Want me to email Kate at Westside PM this welcome note (below)? Reply **send welcome** to send it.

Each email gets its own thread and its own reply words, so a "yes" in the wrong place can never send the wrong thing. If you just say "yes", it asks again with the exact words. If a question ever doesn't make sense, reply "what does that do?" and it has to explain before doing anything. Nothing goes to anyone but you without those words.

## Money
- The partner fee is **5% of the job value, paid once the customer's invoice is settled in full** (your words).
- Still to decide: is that 5% before GST or including GST? The Revenue Partner asks you once. Until you answer, it records paid invoices but works out no fee.
- It proposes the payouts each month. **You** pay them from the business account and tell it "paid".

## When something's stuck
- Revenue Partner quiet? Tell the Workshop "it's gone quiet".
- Anything else stuck for more than a few minutes: send Justin a voice note. Say what you were trying to do and what happened.
