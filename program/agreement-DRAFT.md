## 1. What this is about

You introduce your customers to Smart Contracting's drainage services, mostly through short email or text campaigns that you send to your own customers. We look after anyone who gets in touch. For any job that comes from your introduction, we pay you a fee.

## 2. What you do

1. Send campaigns to your own customers, from your own email or text system, at times we agree.
2. Approve the wording of anything that goes to your customers before it's sent. Nothing goes to your customers without your approval.
3. Send only to people you're allowed to email or text under New Zealand law, including the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007, and include your own business details and a working unsubscribe option in every message.
4. Take people off your list when they ask, as the law requires.
5. Introduce individual customers directly only with that customer's OK.
6. Not make promises on Smart Contracting's behalf about prices, timing or results.

## 3. What Smart Contracting does

1. Writes the campaign email and text for you to check and change.
2. Looks after everyone who gets in touch through your campaign or introduction: a CCTV drain inspection with a written report, and a quote if work is needed.
3. Does the work, and deals with the customer directly about it. The customer's contract for the work is with Smart Contracting, not with you.
4. Keeps a record of every referral against your reference code, and tells you when a referral comes in.
5. Pays your fee as set out below.

3. No fee is owed until the customer's invoice is settled in full. If an invoice is only part paid, the fee waits until it's paid in full.
4. Follow-on work for the same customer: [follow-on work for the same referred customer: to confirm with Jesse].
5. Fees are worked out once a month. We send you a remittance note and pay by bank transfer, with the reference `SC-<your partner id>-<YYYY-MM>`. [payout timing after approval: to confirm with Jesse]
6. You're responsible for your own tax on the fee. If your professional rules require you to tell your clients that you receive a referral fee, you'll do that.

## 5. What counts as a referral

1. A customer counts as your referral if they came through your campaign or your introduction: they quote your reference code, mention your business, respond to your campaign, or you introduce them with their OK.
2. **First introduction wins.** If more than one person introduces the same customer, the first introduction recorded with a reference code counts. If no introduction has a code, we'll look at the records together and agree in good faith. [final say if it's still unclear: to confirm with the lawyer]
3. These don't count: a customer who was already in touch with Smart Contracting before your introduction; a quote that doesn't go ahead; work you subcontract to us (that's priced separately); [how long a referral stays linked to the partner: to confirm with Jesse].

## 6. Privacy

1. **No customer list changes hands.** You keep your list. You send campaigns from your own system, and we never receive, copy or use your list.
2. We only receive a customer's details when that customer contacts us, or when you pass them on with the customer's OK.
3. Each of us looks after personal information under the Privacy Act 2020. We'll only tell you what you need to know about a referral to confirm it and pay your fee, not the details of the customer's job.

## 7. Free to work with others

This agreement doesn't tie either of us to the other. You can recommend other drainage businesses, and Smart Contracting can work with other partners, including others in your line of work.

## 8. Ending it

1. Either of us can end this agreement at any time by email.
2. Fees for jobs introduced before the agreement ends: [fees on jobs introduced before either side ends it: to confirm with Jesse].
3. After it ends, you stop sending campaigns that mention Smart Contracting, and we stop using your name.

## 9. Changes

Any change to this agreement, including the fee, must be agreed in writing (email is fine) by both of us.
