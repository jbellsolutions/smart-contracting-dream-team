# After-call emails

The Revenue Partner drafts these; **Jesse says yes to each one before it's sent**, and it's sent exactly as he saw it. They go only to people who have talked to Jesse, so the fee may appear where it's useful, always in Jesse's exact words.

Fill the `{curly}` parts from the ledger. Never work out an amount yourself: copy amounts and references exactly as `partner_ledger.py` printed them. Keep it short and plain (`voice.md`). Same layout as Jesse's other emails: a blank line before the sign-off.

Sign-off on every email:
```
Cheers,
Jesse
Smart Contracting Ltd | PH 03 567 0876
```

---

## Welcome

When: the partner has said yes and is in the ledger as `signed`.

**Subject:** Welcome aboard, {first name}

> Hi {first name},
>
> Thanks for the chat, and welcome aboard. Here's how it works from here.
>
> 1. We write a short email (and a text version) for your {sellers / past clients / owners / customers}. You check it, change anything that doesn't sound like you, and approve it.
> 2. You send it from your own email or text system. Your list stays with you; we never see it.
> 3. Anyone who gets in touch comes straight to us. We look after them, starting with a CCTV check and a written report.
> 4. For any job that comes from your clients, you get 5% of the job value, paid once the customer's invoice is settled in full.
>
> Your reference code is **{ref_code}**. It goes in your campaign so your clients can quote it, and it's how we match every job to you.
>
> Two quick questions:
> - Which month suits for your first campaign, and roughly how many people are on your list?
> - Are you GST registered? It helps us get your fee right.
>
> Cheers,
> Jesse
> Smart Contracting Ltd | PH 03 567 0876

Notes for the agent: suggest a month if the call didn't settle one ("How does {month} look?"). Don't add a GST sentence about the fee until Jesse decides it: [GST basis: to confirm with Jesse].

## Campaign brief

When: Jesse has said yes to the campaign email for this partner. Goes to the partner, with the campaign email pasted underneath.

**Subject:** Your campaign email, ready for you to check

> Hi {first name},
>
> Here's the email for your {list} (below), plus a short text version if you'd rather send a text. Have a read and change anything that doesn't sound like you. It's your email to your people, so you have the final say.
>
> When you're happy, just reply "approved" (or send me your changes) and it's ready to go from your own system on {date}.
>
> A few things to keep in:
> - It comes from you, so your name and business are on it.
> - Send it only to people you're already allowed to email or text, and keep the unsubscribe line at the bottom.
> - Your code {ref_code} is in there, so anyone who gets in touch is matched to you.
>
> Once it's gone, let me know roughly how many people it went to, and I'll keep you posted on who gets in touch.
>
> Cheers,
> Jesse
> Smart Contracting Ltd | PH 03 567 0876
>
> ---
> {the campaign email and text, exactly as Jesse approved them}

Notes for the agent: if the partner changes the wording, show Jesse the new version before it goes anywhere.

>
> Thanks again for sending your clients our way. Any questions, give me a call.
>
> Cheers,
> Jesse
> Smart Contracting Ltd | PH 03 567 0876

Notes for the agent: never put customer names, addresses or job values in this email (see `compliance-nz.md`). Copy the amount and reference exactly; if the ledger can't produce them, tell Jesse and stop.
