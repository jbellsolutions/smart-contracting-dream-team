# The Smart Contracting partner programme

For Jesse, Mike and the Revenue Partner. One page. The detail is in the files listed at the bottom.

## The idea in one breath

Local businesses whose customers own older Christchurch homes tell those customers about Smart Contracting, usually with one email (or text) campaign to their own list. We look after everyone who gets in touch. For any job that comes from it, the partner gets **5% of the job value, paid once the customer's invoice is settled in full** (Jesse's words; keep them exactly). [GST basis: to confirm with Jesse]

Why it works: older Christchurch homes still have earthenware drains cracked by the quakes, insurance has timed out, the homeowner pays, and "nobody knows about it… it's underground". A partner who mentions a drain check is doing their customers a favour.

## Who partners are

| Tier | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| First (Jesse's picks) | Building inspectors, real-estate agents, property managers | They're there at the moments drains matter: buying, selling, a change of tenants |
| Next | Trades already at the house: electricians, plumbers, builders, roofers | They're in older homes every day and trusted by the owners |
| Later | Local newsletters and community magazines, local influencers, community and event sponsorship, local media | Bigger reach, slower to set up |

Ranking, angles and opening lines: `partner-types.md`.

## What a partner does

- Sends a campaign to **their own** customer list from **their own** email or text system. We write it; they approve it; they send it. Their list never leaves them.
- Rhythm: one campaign at a time, agreed on the call. The aim is one partner campaign going out each month across the programme, so with three or four good partners each list hears from us about once every three months and nobody gets tired of it (Justin's advice on the Oct 7 call, matching the Oct 9 routine draft; Jesse can change it).
- Sends only to people they're allowed to email or text (see `compliance-nz.md`).
- Can also introduce a customer directly, with that customer's OK.

## What Smart Contracting does

- Writes the campaign email and a short text version for the partner to check.
- Handles every response, fast: a call back, then a CCTV drain inspection first, with a written report every time, then a clear quote if anything needs fixing.
- Keeps the partner posted on who's been in touch (without oversharing customer details).
- Records every referral against the partner's reference code, and pays the fee once the customer's invoice is settled in full.

## What the partner approves

**Anything that goes to their customers.** Nothing goes to a partner's list without the partner's yes, and nothing goes to a partner without Jesse's yes. If the wording changes after either yes, it goes back for another look.

## The path

| Step | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1. First reply | They reply to Jesse's cold email. Answer in a line and get it off email: ask for a number and a time | Revenue Partner drafts, Jesse approves |
| 2. Call | Ten minutes. Explain the campaign in plain words. Close: "Cool, let's do a campaign. How many people are on your list?" Leave with a date | Jesse |
| 3. Signed | They say yes. Added to the ledger with a partner ID and reference code (like `SC-P001`). Welcome email | Ledger tool; Revenue Partner drafts, Jesse approves |
| 4. First campaign | Email drafted from the template for their type, Jesse checks it, partner approves it, partner sends it | Revenue Partner, Jesse, partner |
| 5. Referrals | Customers call or email quoting the partner's name or code. Logged straight away | Jesse, Revenue Partner via the ledger |
| 6. Paid | Customer's invoice settled in full → fee owed. Monthly payout proposal, Jesse approves and pays, remittance note | Ledger tool proposes; Jesse pays |

Then keep them warm: a thank-you when a referral comes in, a check-in if they go quiet, and the next campaign idea from `campaign-calendar.md`.

## The rules that never bend

1. The fee is **never** in a cold email. It's for calls, the one-pager (only to people who have replied) and signed partners.
2. Jesse's yes before anything goes to anyone.
3. The partner's yes before anything goes to their customers.
4. The agent never works out a fee in its head (the ledger tool does) and never moves money.
5. No made-up results, prices, testimonials or promises. If we don't know it, we leave a `[… to confirm with Jesse]` gap.

## Sending to our list

Reciprocity is **selective**. Smart Contracting may tell its own customers about a partner only when that partner's work genuinely suits our customers (for example, a plumber who does the kind of plumbing we don't), and only on Jesse's yes for that partner. It is never offered broadly, never part of the standard pitch, and never promised on a first call. If ten partners each got a send to our list, our customers would stop reading.
