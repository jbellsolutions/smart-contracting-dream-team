# Partner campaigns

A campaign is one email (or text) that a **partner sends to their own customers**, introducing Smart Contracting. We write it, the partner approves it, the partner sends it. We never get their list and never send to it.

| Partner type | Template |
|---|---|
| Real-estate agents | `real-estate.md` |
| Building inspectors | `building-inspectors.md` |
| Property managers | `property-managers.md` |
| Trades (electricians, plumbers, builders, roofers and similar) | `trades.md` |

## How a campaign runs

1. **Pick the theme.** From `../campaign-calendar.md` for the month, or Jesse's idea. The seasonal angles below help.
2. **Draft it** from the template for the partner's type. Fill the placeholders (below). Nothing else changes unless Jesse says so.
3. **Jesse's yes.** Jesse sees the whole email and text exactly as the partner would send them.
4. **The partner approves the copy.** It goes to the partner with the "Campaign brief" email (`../emails.md`). They can change the wording; if they do, Jesse sees it again. **Partners approve anything that goes to their customers.**
5. **The partner sends it from their own system**, to people they're allowed to email or text (`../compliance-nz.md`). Their name and business are on it, it says it comes from them, and it carries their unsubscribe line.
6. **Responses come to Smart Contracting.** Customers call 03 567 0876 (or email) and quote the partner's name or reference code. Jesse looks after them: CCTV check first, written report, clear quote.
7. **The ledger records it.** Each response is logged as a referral against the partner's code; the campaign row gets the send date, how many people it reached, replies, bookings and jobs won. Fees follow `../terms.md`.

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Filled with |
|---|---|
| `[partner_name]` | The partner's own name, as they sign their emails |
| `[partner_business]` | Their business name |
| `[ref_code]` | Their reference code from the ledger, e.g. `SC-P001` |
| `[offer]` | What Jesse agreed for this campaign (below) |
| `[partner_contact]` | The partner's phone number or business address, so the sender details are accurate |

The placeholders above (with an underscore) are filled by us before the draft goes to Jesse. `[first name]` (with a space) is different: it's for the **partner's own system**, their mail-merge field, or "Hi there" if they don't use one. Leave that one for the partner.

### About `[offer]`

Jesse sets the offer for each campaign. **There is no discount and no free check unless Jesse names one**, in his own words, for that campaign. Ideas from the calls (a customer discount, a free inspection) were never decided. If Jesse hasn't set an offer, use the default line in the template, which offers nothing beyond a chat.

## What never goes in a campaign

- The partner's fee, or anything about the partner being paid.
- Prices, turnaround promises, guarantees or results we can't back.
- Claims about the reader's own drains or insurance. "Many older homes…" is fine; "your drains are cracked" is not.
- Links, unless the partner asks for one and Jesse agrees.
- Any mention of AI or software.

## The unsubscribe line

Every template ends with an unsubscribe line in the partner's name. It must actually work: replies saying "unsubscribe" (or "STOP" to a text) go to the partner, and **the partner must take that person off their list within 5 working days**. If their email system adds its own unsubscribe link, keep that too. If their text system doesn't handle STOP replies, use the email version instead.

## Seasonal angles

Seasons only. No dates promised. All of these are ideas *(inferred)*, not results.

- **The wet, colder months (late autumn and winter):** blocked gully traps, water pooling, soggy lawns and soak pits that can't keep up. "Find out before the next downpour."
- **Spring selling season:** the busiest time for listings. Pre-sale drain checks for sellers; drains are a classic late surprise in a sale.
- **After a storm or heavy rain:** the moment people notice problems. A gentle "if your drains struggled in the rain…" from a partner is timely and useful. (See `../../radar/storm-day.md`; no fear-selling.)
- **Tenancy changeovers:** summer especially, when many rentals change hands. A drain check between tenants, with a written report for the owner's records.
- **Renovation season:** spring and summer, when owners start bathrooms, kitchens and extensions. A good time to check what's underground before the work goes in.

Avoid tying a campaign to the quake anniversaries. The story matters, but a promotion on those days would land badly.
