# Campaign template: trades

For electricians, plumbers, builders, roofers and similar trades who are already in their customers' homes.

**Who sends it:** the trade business, from their own email or text system.
**Who to:** past customers, especially owners of older homes, as long as the business is allowed to email or text them.
**When:** spring and summer renovation season, before winter, or after heavy rain.

Default `[offer]` line (use this if Jesse hasn't set an offer for this campaign):
> If you're not sure whether you need a check, Jesse's happy to talk it through on the phone first.

Note for the trade: swap in a line about your own work if you like ("We do the wiring, they do the drains"). It's your email to your customers.
