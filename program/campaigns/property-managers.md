# Campaign template: property managers

**Who sends it:** the property manager or their agency, from their own email or text system.
**Who to:** the owners and landlords whose properties they manage, as long as they're allowed to email or text them.
**When:** before winter, at tenancy changeovers (summer especially), and after heavy rain.

Default `[offer]` line (use this if Jesse hasn't set an offer for this campaign):
> If you're not sure whether your property needs a check, Jesse's happy to talk it through on the phone first.

Note for the property manager: this isn't about replacing your usual contractors. It's a way for your owners to find out what condition their drains are in.

---

## Email

**From:** [partner_name], [partner_business]
**Subject:** Do you know what condition your rental's drains are in?
*(Other subject options: "Before winter: a quick word about drains" · "A drain check between tenants")*

> Hi [first name],
>
> Drains are easy to forget because you can't see them. But plenty of older Christchurch rentals still have earthenware drains, some cracked in the earthquakes, and a blocked or broken drain often shows up as an urgent call-out with a tenant in the house.
>
> We've teamed up with Smart Contracting, a local Christchurch drainage team, so our owners can find out where they stand first. They run a camera through the drains and give you a written CCTV report for your records. If anything needs fixing, you get a clear quote before anything is done, and you decide.
>

>
> Kind regards,
