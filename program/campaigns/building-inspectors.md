# Campaign template: building inspectors

**Who sends it:** the inspector or their business, from their own email or text system.
**Who to:** past clients (mostly people who bought, or were about to buy, an older home), as long as the inspector is allowed to email or text them.
**When:** any time. Works well before winter and in the spring selling season.

Default `[offer]` line (use this if Jesse hasn't set an offer for this campaign):
> If you're not sure whether you need a check, Jesse's happy to talk it through on the phone first.

Note for the inspector: change the wording so it sits right with how you present your own reports. It's your email to your clients.

---

## Email

**From:** [partner_name], [partner_business]
**Subject:** The part of your home an inspection can't see
*(Other subject options: "A quick word about your drains" · "Older home? Worth checking the drains")*

> Hi [first name],
>
> When we inspected your home, we looked at everything we could see. Drains are different: they run underground, so they're easy to miss. Plenty of older Christchurch homes still have earthenware drains, and some were cracked in the earthquakes without anyone knowing.
>
> If you'd like peace of mind, I can recommend Smart Contracting, a local Christchurch drainage team. They run a camera through your drains and give you a written CCTV report, so you know exactly what condition they're in. If anything needs fixing, you get a clear quote first.
>
> [offer]
>
> To book a check or ask a question, call Jesse at Smart Contracting on 03 567 0876 and mention [partner_business] (or quote [ref_code]).
>
> Kind regards,
> [partner_name]
> [partner_business]
> [partner_contact]
>
> ---
> This email is from [partner_business]. You're getting it because you're a past client of ours. We haven't shared your details with Smart Contracting or anyone else. If you'd rather not get emails like this from [partner_business], reply with "unsubscribe" and we'll take you off our list.
