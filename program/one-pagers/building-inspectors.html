Why it matters to your clients

Drains run underground, so they're the hardest part of a house to judge on a walk-through. Plenty of older Christchurch homes still have earthenware drains, and many were cracked in the quakes.

Insurance cover for that damage has largely timed out, so the owner pays, and nobody knows until someone looks. A CCTV drain check with a written report sits neatly beside your own report, and gives your clients a clear answer before they commit.
