# Partner one-pagers

Four printable A4 sheets, one per partner type. They follow Jesse's Oct 9 one-pager draft.

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `real-estate.html` | Real-estate agents and offices |
| `building-inspectors.html` | Building inspectors |
| `property-managers.html` | Property managers |
| `trades.html` | Electricians, plumbers, builders, roofers and other trades |

## Who gets one

**Only people who have replied** and asked for something in writing, or who Jesse has talked to. The sheet shows the fee ("5% of the job value, paid once the customer's invoice is settled in full"), so it never goes out with a cold email.

## Before the first one goes out

Each sheet has yellow gaps that print as they are. Fill them in first (edit the HTML file in Notepad, or ask the Workshop to do it):

- `[areas covered: to confirm with Jesse]`: the suburbs and areas you cover.
- `[GST basis: to confirm with Jesse]`: whether the 5% is on the amount before or after GST.
- `[partner email address: to confirm with Jesse]`: the email address partners should use.

If you'd like a starting price on the sheet, tell the Workshop. Right now the sheets say "we quote per job, after a CCTV check", with no prices.

## Print to PDF in Edge

1. Double-click the file to open it in Edge.
2. Press **Ctrl+P**.
3. **Printer:** choose **Save as PDF**.
4. **Layout:** Portrait. **Paper size:** A4. **Margins:** Default. **Scale:** 100.
5. Under **More settings**, untick **Headers and footers** and tick **Background graphics** (so the coloured boxes print).
6. Click **Save** and name it, e.g. `Smart Contracting - partner sheet.pdf`.

It should come out as exactly one page. If it ever spills onto a second page, set **Scale** to 95 and save again.

## Changing the wording

Ask the Workshop, so all four sheets stay in step. Keep the fee line exactly as it is. No prices, results, reviews or guarantees unless Jesse supplies them.
