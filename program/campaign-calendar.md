# Campaign calendar: ideas by month

**These are ideas, not promises.** Seasons only; nothing here commits anyone to a date, a result or an offer. They're our reasoning about a Canterbury drainage business *(inferred)*, not data. Change them as real campaigns show what works.

Use them to pick a campaign theme (the ledger's `--theme`) and a partner type. NZ seasons: summer is December to February, autumn March to May, winter June to August, spring September to November.

| Month | Theme idea | Best partner types | The angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **January** | Tenancy changeover check | Property managers | Many rentals change hands over summer. A drain check between tenants, with a written report for the owner. |
| **February** | Settling into an older home | Building inspectors, real-estate (past buyers) | People who bought in spring and summer are settling in. "Do you know what's under your new place?" |
| **March** | Autumn listings | Real-estate | The autumn selling window. A pre-sale drain check before listing. |
| **April** | Before the weather turns | Property managers, trades | Leaves and the first heavy rains. Check gully traps, downpipes and soak pits before winter. |
| **May** | Pre-winter drain check | All first-tier partners | Find out before the wet months whether the drains can cope. |
| **June** | Winter: drains under pressure | Property managers, trades | Pooling water, soggy lawns, slow drains. "If your drains struggled in the last rain…" |
| **July** | Plan the fix for spring | Trades, building inspectors | A quieter time to get a CCTV check and plan any relay before the busy season. |
| **August** | Get ready to sell | Real-estate | Owners thinking of listing in spring. Get the drains checked now so there are no surprises later. |
| **September** | Spring selling season | Real-estate, building inspectors | The busiest listing season. A drain problem found late can hold up a sale. |
| **October** | Spring selling (continued) and renovation planning | Real-estate, trades | Pre-sale checks, plus owners planning bathrooms, kitchens and extensions. |
| **November** | Renovation season and end-of-year tenancies | Trades, property managers | Check what's underground before the work goes in. Student and end-of-year rentals start to turn over. |
| **December** | Before the break | Property managers, past customers of trades | A check before the holidays. Keep it light; many partners won't want to send in the last weeks of the year. |

## Any time

- **After a storm or heavy rain.** The best moment to be useful. A short, helpful note from a partner ("if your drains struggled in the rain…"), never fear-selling. See `../radar/storm-day.md`.
- **When a partner has a run of older listings or inspections.** Their timing beats the calendar.

## Avoid

- Tying a campaign to the earthquake anniversaries (September and February). The story matters, but a promotion on those days would land badly.
- Sending to the same partner's list too often. Their customers are their relationship; when in doubt, wait.
- Seasonal claims we can't back ("most drains fail in winter"). Say "can", "often", "worth checking".
